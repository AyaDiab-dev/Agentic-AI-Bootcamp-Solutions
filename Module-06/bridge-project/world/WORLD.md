THE HOLLOW - world canon, as things stood when the party first arrived

A small river village of forty houses, a mill, a forge, a shrine on the hill
and one inn. Cosy, low-stakes, a little odd. Nothing here wants to kill anyone.

PLACES
- The Lantern: the only inn. Run by Pell, a cheerful innkeeper who knows everyone's business. Rooms are two coppers a night.
- The forge: by the river, downstream of the mill. Smoke from its chimney is the first thing travellers see.
- The green door: the herbalist's shop, on the square. Bundles of feverleaf and lavender hang in the window.
- The mill: upstream, wheel turning. The miller keeps a careful ledger of every sack that passes through.
- The shrine: on the hill above the village. A stone altar under an open roof. Villagers leave small offerings there.
- The toll bridge: the only crossing over the river to the east road. A toll is collected, though nobody in the village will say by whom.
- The ferry: a mile downstream, a flat-bottomed boat run by a taciturn woman named Sela. Rarely used while the bridge stands.

PEOPLE
- Marra, the smith: broad, soot-sleeved, kind, blunt. Fixes anything with a wheel or a blade. Lives above the forge.
- Mirra, the herbalist: small, quick, gossipy, precise. Runs the green door. Not related to Marra, and tired of being asked.
- Old Tobb, the miller: anxious, honest, proud of his ledger. Widowed. Feeds every stray in the village.
- Pell, the innkeeper: sees all, tells most.
- Sela, the ferrywoman: says little, misses nothing.

THINGS
- The Tidewater amulet: a blue glass pendant left on the shrine's altar generations ago. Villagers say it keeps the river from flooding. Nobody has ever moved it.
- The miller's ledger: Tobb's leather-bound record. He is lost without it.

RUMOURS (things people say; not confirmed)
- Someone calling himself the Toll-King collects the bridge toll and keeps it.
- The shrine offerings have been going missing.

TONE
Warm, gentle, a little funny. Trouble is small and fixable. Nobody is a villain for long.
