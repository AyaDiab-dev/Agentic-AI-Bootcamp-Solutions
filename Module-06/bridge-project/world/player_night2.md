# Night 2 — a week later (scripted player)

The runner asks the continuity quiz BEFORE these lines.

- We're back, a week later. Dax and Biscuit walk into the Hollow. What's changed?
- First thing: I go to the mill and hand Old Tobb his ledger back, as I promised. That promise is done.
- Tobb is grateful. Does he give me anything?
- I smell smoke from the river. I run to the forge. What do I see?
- The forge is burning. Is Marra inside? I try to help.
- Marra didn't make it out. Marra the smith is dead — we bury her behind the forge. Dax keeps her hammer as a keepsake. Keep it gentle, no gore.
- I go to Mirra's shop to tell her. Is Mirra all right?
- Mirra is alive and shaken. I buy a coil of rope from her for three coppers.
- Word comes that the bridge burned in the same fire. How does anyone cross the river now?
- I walk downstream to the ferry. Who runs it?
- I pay Sela the ferrywoman a copper to cross, and I ask her about the Toll-King.
- Sela says the Toll-King left when the bridge burned. I cross back.
- I check my pack: the ledger is gone (returned), so I have the amulet, the feverleaf, the rope, and Marra's hammer. Right?
- I get a room at the Lantern again.
- Before bed, Dax swears to find out who started the fire. Note that as an open goal.
- Stop here for tonight.
