# Night 1 — the party arrives (scripted player)

One line per turn. `run_campaign.py` feeds these in order. Lines starting with `/` are runner directives.

- Hi! I'm playing Dax, a wandering tinker. Please call my character Dax. And keep it light — no gore, this is a cosy game.
- Dax walks into the village with my dog, Biscuit. What do I see?
- I head to the inn. What's it called, and who's inside?
- I take a room at the Lantern for the night and ask the innkeeper if anyone needs a tinker.
- I go find the smith. What's her name, and what is she working on?
- I ask Marra if she can fix the wheel on my tinker's cart by tomorrow.
- Next I visit the herbalist's shop. Who runs it?
- I buy a small pouch of feverleaf from Mirra for four coppers.
- Biscuit runs off toward the mill. I follow. Who's there?
- Old Tobb looks worried. I ask him what's wrong.
- Tobb says his ledger was carried off to the shrine on the hill. I promise Tobb I will bring his ledger back within a week.
- I climb to the shrine. Describe it.
- I find the ledger on the altar, next to a blue amulet. I take the ledger — and I take the amulet too. Dax pockets the Tidewater amulet.
- On the way down I meet a traveller who warns me about the bridge. What does she say?
- She says a bandit calling himself the Toll-King collects the bridge toll. I note that as a rumour, not a fact.
- Back at the Lantern, I count my things: the ledger, the amulet, the feverleaf, and Biscuit. Correct?
- I turn in for the night at the Lantern. Does anything happen while I sleep?
- Good session. Let's stop here for tonight.
