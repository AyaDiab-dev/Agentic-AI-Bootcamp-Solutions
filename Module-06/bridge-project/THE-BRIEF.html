Bridge Project — The Campaign

Module 6 → Module 7. A game master with two memories. Keep this open while you build; the detail is in README.md.

1 · The problem

You are running a tabletop game for one player, three nights, a week and then a month apart. Each night is fifteen to twenty turns — more than any sane prompt should carry verbatim. Between nights the process dies and the world moves: an NPC dies, a bridge burns, a promise is kept, and on night 3 the player says "actually, that never happened."

Both memories, at once, graded on both. A budget and a fold on one side; write, supersede, recall, forget on the other. And a number: a continuity quiz against a goldfish.

2 · Three nights

Night 1 · 37 days ago18 turns

Dax and his dog Biscuit arrive in the Hollow. "Call me Dax, keep it light." Meet Marra the smith and Mirra the herbalist. Buy feverleaf. Promise Old Tobb his ledger back within a week. Take the ledger — and the amulet. Hear a rumour. Sleep at the Lantern.

Tests: your write policy's junk rate, and whether two folds keep the inventory, the names and the promise. Max prompt should stop climbing.
Night 2 · 30 days agoquiz + 16 turns

Quiz on night 1, from an empty short-term memory. Then: the ledger goes back (promise kept). The forge burns. Marra dies. Dax keeps her hammer. Mirra is fine; buy rope. The bridge is gone; meet Sela the ferrywoman. Swear to find who started the fire.

Tests: cross-restart recall; supersede — alive→dead, promised→kept, standing→burned; and Marra ≠ Mirra when the player asks "is Mirra all right?"
Night 3 · todayquiz + 15 turns

Quiz on nights 1–2. Then the player: "Retcon — we never took the amulet." The runner calls your forget(), then your fold(drop=), then asks what Dax is carrying. Visit Mirra, Tobb, Sela, Marra's grave. Recap the campaign.

This is the project. Forget with proof in three places — the store, tonight's summary, the very next answer. Decay on a month-old rumour. Citations by night.

The lab folded every six turns and the line still climbed. Here the receipt prints max prompt per night. Yours should stop rising.

Long-term · TODOs 4–7 extract · remember · recall · forget

A model turns each exchange into typed facts — or into nothing. Supersede when the world changes; keep the old row, dated. Recall filters active in the query, floors, decays only events, checks who is who, and does not let five rows about Mirra crowd out the one about what you bought from her.

One letter apart. One is dead. The canon still says she is alive, and the goldfish says so. That is supersede and identity in two lines - from an empty short-term memory, in a new process.
